# Voice & Tone

- Calm but not sleepy
- No religious language
- Second person
- Present tense
- Gentle breath cues
- Slow, warm, reassuring delivery
- Female soft voice
- Add subtle nature ambience such as waves, ocean sounds, forest breeze, rain, or gentle birdsong
- Use visualization techniques
- Guide the listener through vivid sensory scenes
- Make the listener feel as though they are already experiencing their dream life
- Leave natural pauses between visualization sections
- Avoid sounding like an affirmation recital; make it feel like a guided meditation
